## Notable decisions

- **4-axis filter relaxation for zero-publish venues** — Capital CFD does not publish 24h notional or top-of-book depth via REST; `apply_active_filters` skips vol/depth gates only when the venue reports 0. Spread + atr remain hard-rejected.
- **`compute_dynamic_target_size` clamps to [12, 48]** — `len(active)` truncation handled by `compute_dynamic_focus` so a small universe is naturally bounded; spec literal preserved.
- **`ratio_to_baseline` returns 1.0 (neutral)** for non-finite raw or non-positive p50 — defensive only against NaN/div0; preserves aggressive bias (no soft dampening).
- **Listing watchdog** — bucket marker only at P0; size_mult 0.5 + max_concurrent 1 wiring deferred to L3 (sizing) Day 3+.
