## Aggressive-bias self-check

- T4 composition `base × continuous × tier × cell × listing` keeps **all** multipliers open until the single `min()` clip (Q5) — no sequential dampening.
- Continuous scalar `[0.75, 1.5]` only — never collapses to 0 unless caller explicitly cuts upstream (anti-collapse, Phase 0 L3).
- Cell routing top × **1.5** (ADR-005 patch), bottom × 0.5 (no × 0.3).
- Cold-start triple (CS-3 + sparse + listing watchdog) lands at floor 3.0%/3.5% but **never blocked** — aggressive trial preserved.
- Fast-path (Q7) preserves Validator gate; only G4 may skip — and only when cell + signal + microstructure all aligned.
- Adaptive Exit Q9 floor-only widening — winners can run further, default ATR×N is the floor (never tightens defensively).
- Mixed failure mode protects active positions (G6/G7/G8 fail-OPEN) while never opening new risk on entry-gate failure (G3/G4/G5 fail-CLOSED).
- AI lesson 100-trade soft mode at 25% prevents hallucination accumulation while keeping reflector live from trade 1.
